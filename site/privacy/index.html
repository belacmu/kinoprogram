Privacy

Last updated 23 November 2026

Cinecrab is a small personal project that lists the films showing in a few cities, by film. You can browse it without an account and nothing about you is stored anywhere but your own browser. This page explains what changes if you sign in.

Without an account

Your watchlist, hidden films, filters and chosen city are kept in your browser's local storage on your device. They are not sent to us. There are no ads, no analytics and no tracking cookies.

With an account

You can sign in with a one-time code sent to your email, or with Google. We store:

  • Your email address, to sign you in and, if you turn it on, to send the daily email.
  • Your name, if you give one or if Google provides it. It is shown to your friends and to people going to the same showing.
  • Your watchlist, hidden films and settings, so they sync across your devices.
  • Friends you add by code, and the showings you mark yourself as going to (with seats, if you enter them).

When you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address and profile picture from Google, and use only the name and email. We don't get access to your Gmail, contacts, calendar or anything else in your Google account.

Who can see what

  • Friends you've added see your name and the films on your watchlist.
  • When you mark yourself as going to a showing, your friends and the people in that showing's group (through invite links) can see that you're going, and your seats if you entered them.
  • Nobody else sees your email address. We never sell or share your data with anyone else.

Services we use

  • Supabase stores accounts and the data above, on servers in the EU.
  • Google (Gmail) sends the sign-in codes and the daily email. Google sign-in, if you choose it.
  • GitHub Pages hosts the site. Fonts come from Google Fonts, film posters from TMDB and the sign-in library from jsDelivr, so those services see your IP address when the page loads, as with any website.
  • hCaptcha may check that sign-ins come from a person, not a bot.

Emails

The daily email is off until you turn it on. Every email has a one-click unsubscribe link, and you can turn it off under Account.

Deleting your data

Email cinecrabapp@gmail.com from the address you signed in with and we'll delete your account and everything attached to it. To remove Cinecrab's access to your Google account, go to myaccount.google.com/connections.

Contact
